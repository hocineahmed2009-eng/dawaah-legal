Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Projet Dawaah collects, uses, and protects information when you use our TikTok content automation application.

Effective: September 2026 Version 1.0 TikTok Integration

1. About This Application

Projet Dawaah is an automated content publishing tool designed to distribute Islamic educational content (dourous) to TikTok. The application is operated by an individual developer and is used solely for the purpose of publishing pre-approved religious educational material.

2. Information We Access

Through the TikTok API integration, our application accesses only what is necessary to post content on your behalf:

  • TikTok account identifier (to authorize posting)
  • Content metadata (video descriptions, hashtags, captions)
  • Basic account information required by the TikTok API

We do not collect, store, or process personal data from TikTok viewers or followers.

3. How We Use This Information

The information accessed is used exclusively to:

  • Authenticate your TikTok account for automated posting
  • Schedule and publish pre-approved Islamic educational video content
  • Monitor the status of posted content via the TikTok API

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with any third party for commercial purposes.

4. Data Storage and Security

API credentials and access tokens are stored locally on a private server and are never shared with unauthorized parties. We implement standard security practices to prevent unauthorized access. Authentication tokens are refreshed periodically and stored in an encrypted format.

5. Third-Party Services

This application integrates with the TikTok API. Your use of TikTok is governed by TikTok's own Privacy Policy. We do not control TikTok's data practices.

6. Your Rights

You may revoke this application's access to your TikTok account at any time through TikTok's security settings under "Manage app permissions." Upon revocation, we will no longer have access to your account.

7. Content Policy

All content published through this application consists of pre-approved Islamic educational material. No content is generated or published without prior human review and authorization from the relevant religious scholar.
